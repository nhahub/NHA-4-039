# Stakeholder Analysis

## 1. Purpose
This document identifies the key stakeholders for the Olist e-commerce analytics project, their roles, needs, and success metrics (KPIs).

## 2. Stakeholder Map

| Stakeholder | Role | Needs / Questions | KPIs |
|-------------|------|-------------------|------|
| **CEO / Executive** | Strategic decision maker | Overall business performance, growth trends | Total Revenue, Revenue Trend, Monthly Growth Rate |
| **Sales Manager** | Sales team leader | Top products, categories, revenue drivers | Revenue by Category, Top 10 Products, AOV |
| **Operations Manager** | Logistics head | Delivery performance, bottlenecks | Avg Delivery Days, On-Time Delivery Rate, Delayed Orders % |
| **Marketing Manager** | Customer acquisition & retention | Customer behavior, loyalty, segments | Repeat Customer Rate, New vs Repeat, RFM Segments |
| **Logistics Manager** | Shipping optimization | Freight costs, regional performance | Freight Ratio, Freight by State, Avg Freight per Order |
| **Sellers** | Marketplace vendors | Individual performance, benchmarks | Seller Rating, Seller Lead Time, Seller Revenue Rank |
| **Product Manager** | Product strategy | Category performance, product insights | Top/Bottom Categories, Price Distribution, Product Volume |

## 3. Stakeholder Questions (Detailed)

### 3.1 CEO / Executive
- "What is our total revenue and growth trend?"
- "Which states generate the most revenue?"
- "What is our customer satisfaction level?"

### 3.2 Sales Manager
- "Which categories generate the most revenue?"
- "Which products are top sellers by volume?"
- "What is the average order value?"

### 3.3 Operations Manager
- "How can we reduce delivery delays?"
- "Which states have the worst delivery performance?"
- "What is the on-time delivery rate?"

### 3.4 Marketing Manager
- "What is our repeat customer rate?"
- "How are customers distributed across states?"
- "What is the review score distribution?"

### 3.5 Logistics Manager
- "Which states have the highest freight costs?"
- "What is the freight-to-revenue ratio?"
- "Are freight costs correlated with delivery delays?"

### 3.6 Sellers
- "How do I compare to other sellers?"
- "What is my average review score?"
- "What is my lead time compared to average?"

### 3.7 Product Manager
- "Which categories have the best reviews?"
- "Which categories have the worst reviews?"
- "What is the price distribution by category?"


## 4. Stakeholder Communication Plan

| Stakeholder | Communication Frequency | Format | Channel |
|-------------|------------------------|--------|---------|
| CEO | Weekly | Executive summary + dashboard | Email + Power BI |
| Sales Manager | Weekly | Category & product report | Power BI |
| Operations Manager | Daily | Delivery alerts (if real-time) | Dashboard |
| Marketing Manager | Bi-weekly | Customer segment report | PDF + Dashboard |
| Logistics Manager | Weekly | Freight analysis report | Power BI |
| Sellers | Monthly | Seller performance report | Dashboard (filtered) |
| Product Manager | Weekly | Category insights | Power BI |
