# Functional Requirements

Based on the 18 SQL questions answered in this project:

## Sales & Revenue
- FR-01: Calculate total revenue from delivered orders
- FR-02: Identify top 10 product categories by revenue
- FR-03: Identify top 10 products by number of sales
- FR-04: Identify bottom 5 product categories by revenue
- FR-05: Calculate Average Order Value (AOV)

## Geographic Analysis
- FR-06: Identify top 5 states by revenue
- FR-07: Identify top 5 cities by number of orders
- FR-08: Identify top 5 states with highest cancellation rates
- FR-09: Calculate average delivery days by state

## Reviews & Ratings
- FR-10: Show review score distribution
- FR-11: Identify category with most 5-star reviews
- FR-12: Analyze relationship between delivery time and review score
- FR-13: Identify category with most low ratings (1 or 2)

## Payments
- FR-14: Identify most used payment method
- FR-15: Calculate average installments by payment method
- FR-16: Identify payment method with highest revenue

## Delivery
- FR-17: Calculate average actual delivery time (days)
- FR-18: Analyze delayed orders (count and revenue impact)

## Forecasting
- FR-19: Forecast monthly sales for next 6 months using Linear Regression

## Dashboard
- FR-20: Provide interactive Power BI dashboard with 5 pages
