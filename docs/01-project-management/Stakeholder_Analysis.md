# Stakeholder Analysis

## Stakeholder Map

| Stakeholder | Role | Needs | KPIs |
|-------------|------|-------|------|
| CEO | Executive decision maker | Overall performance | Total Revenue, Trend |
| Sales Manager | Sales team leader | Top products/categories | Revenue by Category, Top Products |
| Operations Manager | Logistics head | Delivery performance | Avg Delivery Days, On-Time Rate |
| Marketing Manager | Customer acquisition | Customer behavior | Repeat Rate, New vs Repeat |
| Logistics Manager | Shipping optimization | Shipping costs | Freight Ratio, Freight by State |
| Sellers | Marketplace vendors | Individual performance | Seller Rating, Seller Lead Time |

## Key Questions by Stakeholder

| Stakeholder | Key Question |
|-------------|--------------|
| CEO | "What is our total revenue and growth trend?" |
| Sales Manager | "Which categories generate the most revenue?" |
| Operations Manager | "How can we reduce delivery delays?" |
| Marketing Manager | "What is our repeat customer rate?" |
| Logistics Manager | "Which states have the highest freight costs?" |
| Sellers | "How do I compare to other sellers?" |
